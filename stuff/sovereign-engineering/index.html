Sovereign Engineering.

Cohort for protocol builders. Learn to build on bitcoin and nostr with practitioners in Madeira.

Run with Gigi. Freedom tech has never been short on ideas — conferences, whitepapers, and manifestos it has in abundance. What it's short on is builders. Sovereign Engineering exists to make more of them: six weeks in Madeira, six rotating captains, demo day every Friday. “No talking without shipping” isn't a house rule, it's the whole thesis — nobody gets argued into a freer future; you ship it in front of them.

That's why the format is what it is. Working software every Friday, in a room full of people doing the same, compounds in a way no conference ever has.

format

  • 6-week intensive cohort
  • In-person in Madeira
  • Focus on shipping real software
  • Bitcoin and nostr protocols
  • Peer learning with practitioners

Not a bootcamp. A cohort of builders who want to work on hard problems together.
