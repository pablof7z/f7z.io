Wikifreedia.

Wikipedia on nostr. Collaborative knowledge without central editorial control.

Wikifreedia reimagines collaborative knowledge building on a censorship-resistant substrate. Articles are events, edits are signed, and reputation emerges from the web of trust rather than administrator hierarchies. Truth doesn't come from consensus; embrace the bias, sign your edits, let the web of trust sort it out.

features

  • djot article format
  • Custom relay support
  • In-browser editor
  • Mentions and reactions
  • Version history through event chains
  • Author attribution and reputation

philosophy

  • No central editorial board
  • Trust-based content ranking
  • Forkable knowledge bases
  • Censorship-resistant by design

“If truth by consensus is not the way, if humans are unavoidably biased, the best stuff would come from embracing and doubling down on the biases.”
