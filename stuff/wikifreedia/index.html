Wikifreedia

wikifreedia.xyz · github

Wikipedia on nostr. Collaborative knowledge without central editorial control.

Wikifreedia reimagines collaborative knowledge building on a censorship-resistant substrate. Articles are events, edits are signed, and reputation emerges from the web of trust rather than administrator hierarchies.

features

  • Asciidoc article format
  • Custom relay support
  • In-browser editor
  • Mentions and reactions
  • Version history through event chains
  • Author attribution and reputation

philosophy

  • No central editorial board
  • Trust-based content ranking
  • Forkable knowledge bases
  • Censorship-resistant by design
