Honeypot.

NIP-60 wallet for subscription management. Like Apple's Subscriptions app, but for nostr.

Honeypot manages recurring payments using NIP-88 subscriptions. Track what you're supporting, cancel or adjust, all from one interface.

features

  • View all active subscriptions
  • Manage recurring payments
  • Cancel with one tap
  • Payment history

Based on ideas from Highlighter. Currently in TestFlight.
