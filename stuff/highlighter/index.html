Highlighter.

Creator economy platform for nostr. Read, highlight, share. Long-form content with social annotations and subscriptions.

Highlighter implements NIP-84 highlights — the ability to select and share meaningful excerpts from articles, podcasts, and videos. It turns passive reading into active curation.

features

  • NIP-84 highlight creation and discovery
  • Zap splits for content creators
  • NIP-88 recurring subscriptions
  • DVM (Data Vending Machine) integration
  • NIP-29 groups for communities
  • Curation-based earnings

creator tools

  • Subscriber management
  • Revenue analytics
  • Tiered access levels
  • Community spaces
