Rocking Life.

Built for when I'm overwhelmed, not when I'm not.

Rocking Life (born as Win the Day) is a voice-first execution app: say what you'll do out loud, and it holds you to it. No points, no streaks, no gamification — today's three commitments and one next action, nothing else.

The rule that governs every screen: the app must stay useful when you're overwhelmed, not just when you're not. Fewer choices than a normal task app. One next action instead of a list of options. Missed work is input, not failure — recovery is visible but never dramatic.

what it does

  • One voice entry point: narrate messily, it interprets, you confirm before it applies
  • Today: one state signal, a maximum of three commitments, one optional flex item
  • Week: pattern only, no daily detail unless you ask for it
  • Threads: open loops treated as active background work, not backlog debt

Most todo apps assume you're already on top of things — that the hard part is remembering, not deciding, and that a longer list is a more honest one. Rocking Life assumes the opposite: on a bad day, more choices make things worse, and the app's job is to hand you exactly one next action and get out of the way.

“I don't want to track time of tasks, priorities, complex hierarchical organizations... I just want to track stuff I need to do.” Tools should match your cognition, not some productivity guru's.
