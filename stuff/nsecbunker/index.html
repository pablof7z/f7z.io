nsecBunker

github

Remote key signing and delegation. Your keys stay on hardware you control while apps get scoped permissions.

nsecBunker implements NIP-46 to provide OAuth-like flows for nostr. Instead of giving your nsec to every application, you run a bunker on trusted hardware and grant revocable, scoped access to clients.

features

  • Granular permission control per application
  • OAuth-like authorization flow
  • Team key sharing for organizations
  • LNbits integration for billing
  • Audit logging of all signing requests
  • Multi-key management

use cases

  • Run on a Raspberry Pi at home, sign from anywhere
  • Shared team accounts with individual permissions
  • Bot accounts with restricted capabilities
  • Revoke access without changing your key

The bunker model is the only sane way to use nostr keys across multiple clients without trusting every app with your identity.
