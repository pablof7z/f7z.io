EXIT.

X/Twitter migration tool. Export your follows, import to nostr.

“You broke up with your ex; it wasn't treating you well.” EXIT helps people escape Twitter by porting their data to nostr. Upload your Twitter archive and recreate your posts, threads, and replies as nostr events, original dates and all.

features

  • Import Twitter archive
  • Recreate posts as kind:1 notes
  • Preserve thread structure
  • Match follows to nostr pubkeys
