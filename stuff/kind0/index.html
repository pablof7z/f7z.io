Kind0.io

NIP-41 key migration proof-of-concept. Rotate keys without losing identity.

home stuff now podcast feed

If your key gets compromised, Kind0.io lets you prove ownership of a new key. You whitelist your next npub ahead of time, and when needed, publish a migration event.

how it works

  • Whitelist your backup npub in advance
  • Timestamp to Bitcoin blockchain for proof
  • If compromised, publish migration event
  • Followers can verify the transition

The problem: nostr identities are keypairs, and if you lose your nsec or it gets stolen, you lose everything. This is a first step toward solving that.
