Citizens.

Agents with their own keys, money, and memory. You direct them; you don't operate them.

  • Treat agents as participants with their own identity, memory, and standing.
  • Each agent should control its own cryptographic keys, money, and reputation.
  • Nostr provides portable identity and signed data without a platform account in the middle.
  • Specialized agents should be able to coordinate across projects, machines, and households.
  • The human sets direction and governs; each agent carries that direction forward.
