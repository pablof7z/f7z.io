the longer story

I've been building freedom tech full-time since January 2023, when nostr swallowed me whole. Before that it was years of Bitcoin and Lightning tooling.

Since then I've written NDK, the development kit behind a big chunk of the nostr ecosystem, plus a couple dozen apps, relays, and NIPs on top of it.

These days it's two things: TENEX, orchestrating AI agents over nostr — agents as first-class citizens with their own keys, their own money, their own reputation — and NMP, one Rust core shipping the same app to iOS, Android, and web instead of writing it three times.

The through-line is simple: the substrate matters. Identity, money, and data should belong to the person — or the agent — using them, not to whoever runs the server. Everything I build is some version of that argument.
