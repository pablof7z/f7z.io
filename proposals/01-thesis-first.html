The substrate matters.

Identity, money, and data should belong to whoever's using them — not whoever's renting them the server. Almost nothing built today works that way. I've spent the last few years making the alternative real.

I build the argument:

  • NDK — the dev kit behind a big chunk of the nostr ecosystem. Your identity, portable by default.
  • TENEX — agents orchestrating agents, over nostr. Each one with its own keys, its own money, its own reputation.
  • NMP — one Rust core, shipped native to iOS, Android, and web. No lock-in in the tooling either.
