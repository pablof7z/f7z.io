homepage first-touch proposals

ten takes on replacing the wall-of-bio. open each, resize, toggle dark mode.

  1. Thesis-first manifesto Lead with the argument ("the substrate matters"), person second. The bio disappears.
  2. Artifact ledger Show, don't tell — the homepage is a ledger of shipped things; identity emerges from the work.
  3. Progressive disclosure One line → three labeled facts → <details> with the longer story. Current aesthetic, done right.
  4. Terminal whoami Interactive terminal: auto-types whoami, tappable commands reveal depth on demand.
  5. Q&A Answers the questions visitors silently arrive with — who / what / why / why trust you / where.
  6. Changelog-as-homepage The bio told as git log — reverse-chron one-liners from Bitcoin tooling to HEAD: TENEX + NMP.
  7. Substrate diagram The thesis drawn, not written: identity/money/data as the substrate, projects placed on top.
  8. Index card Everything above the fold as labeled fields — NAME / THESIS / NOW / SHIPPED / BEFORE / FIND. Zero paragraphs.
  9. Typographic poster The bold one: massive editorial type — "Identity. Money. Data. Yours." — resolving into a quiet footer.
  10. Personal note The anti-résumé: three warm sentences signed "— Pablo", then three quiet links. Nothing else.
