I've been building freedom tech full-time since January 2023, when I fell down the nostr rabbit hole and never climbed back out — before that it was years of Bitcoin and Lightning tooling (and one exchange rugging me out of 2 BTC, which taught me more about counterparty risk than any whitepaper). I wrote NDK, the development kit behind a big chunk of the nostr ecosystem, and a couple dozen apps, relays, and NIPs on top of it. These days I'm obsessed with two things: TENEX, orchestrating AI agents over nostr — agents as first-class citizens with their own keys, their own money, their own reputation — and NMP, a Rust-core architecture for shipping the same app to iOS, Android, and web without writing everything three times. The through-line is simple: the substrate matters. Identity, money, and data should belong to the person (or the agent) using them, not to whoever runs the server. Everything I build is some version of that argument.
