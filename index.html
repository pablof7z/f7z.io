Pablo Fernandez (pablof7z). Since January 2023 I've been building, full-time, the infrastructure for closing that distance — on nostr, because anything that remembers you for years needs an identity no company can revoke. Before that: Bitcoin and Lightning tooling, and an exchange that rug-pulled 2 BTC out from under me, which settled the counterparty-risk question permanently.

Grew up in Argentina, coding since before I could drive — left alone at seventeen when the economy collapsed, landed in Spain nearly broke. A decade of unglamorous work followed: an AI research post, corporate infra, a startup that didn't make it, arbitrage trading that paid the bills and taught me what work without meaning feels like. A stubborn meditation practice and a family that's worldschooled across three continents are the reason any of the rest of this got built.
