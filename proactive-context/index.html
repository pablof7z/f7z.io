Deviation.

The summary meant to preserve your thinking is where your thinking quietly disappears.

  • Generic model output tends toward an average answer.
  • Human corrections, convictions, and judgment are the scarce signal in the loop.
  • Generated summaries can erase the nuance and voice that made that signal useful.
  • pc stores the human’s literal words locally instead of paraphrasing them.
  • It returns those exact words to the assistant when later work makes them relevant.
  • The goal is to preserve human direction as agents generate more of the implementation.
