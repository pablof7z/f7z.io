# Episode: Essay argument restructured from pure-geometry position paper to evidence-led report

## Prior State

User's argument was a pure geometry of software fit — ideal points, illustrative 50/85/100 percentages, and an axiom that audience-of-one tools achieve 100% alignment. It contained zero concrete app examples despite being motivated by rebuilt apps, rebutted the weak objection ('custom software preserves irrational processes'), asserted 'fully directable' without operational teeth, and provided no 'why now' to explain what changed to make audience-of-one software newly actionable.

## Trigger

Assistant's developmental read identified five specific structural weaknesses: (1) no concrete app examples means the argument is a position paper not a report, (2) invented 50/85/100 numbers are load-bearing but invite readers to argue about numbers instead of claims, (3) no 'why now' leaves a hole since the fit axiom was always true but never actionable, (4) rebutting the irrational-process objection is a strawman — the real objection is unamortized stewardship burden, (5) 'fully directable' is an assertion lacking the concrete concept of translation depth.

## Decision

Proposed spine replaces the prior structure: open with one specific app mismatch told concretely, earn the geometry through 'the aggregate optimum is a point where nobody actually lives' rather than invented percentages, separate specificity from change-authority as independent axes, make a bounded 'why now' claim using only personal evidence (n=1 direct experience that the threshold has moved far enough for one person), directly confront the stewardship/maintenance objection rather than dodging it, and give 'directable' teeth via translation depth (user→tool vs user→support→PM→designer→engineer→roadmap).

## Consequences

- 50/85/100 percentages are to be replaced or demoted; the 'point where nobody lives' becomes the primary geometric insight
- Essay cannot be drafted until user provides concrete app list and identifies the best specific mismatch story for the opening
- AI-makes-software-cheap claim must be bounded to personal evidence, not generalized from mixed METR/DORA findings
- Stewardship burden must be addressed head-on with the user's actual answer rather than dismissed
- 'Fully directable' must be operationalized as translation depth, not left as an abstract property
