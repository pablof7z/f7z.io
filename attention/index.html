Attention.

The scarcest resource in the universe. Tokens are buyable; your hours are not.

  • Human attention is finite; compute and model tokens can be bought again.
  • Evaluate software by whose attention each interaction spends.
  • Machine attention is finite too: relevant context beats more context.
  • Spend computation freely when it prevents repeated human corrections or manual work.
  • Notifications, refreshes, re-logins, and repeated questions are avoidable attention costs.
