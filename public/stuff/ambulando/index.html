Ambulando

github

Voice-first social network iOS app. 60-second voice messages on nostr with trust-based feed.

Ambulando is about voice, not text. Record short audio messages, share them with your web of trust, discover people through their voice.

features

  • 60-second voice messages
  • Trust-based feed (WoT)
  • Audio-first discovery
  • Native iOS

The name comes from Latin "ambulando solvitur" — it is solved by walking (and talking).
